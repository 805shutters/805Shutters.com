## Summary of the operation

"805 Shutters and Shades" (805shuttersandshades.com, listing phone 805-870-8330)
is **not a Ventura County business**. It is a January-2026 re-skin of
**818 Shutters and Shades LLC** (Calabasas, LA County, CSLB #1147886, qualifier
Richard Mwesige), part of a templated multi-market network branded
"Area Code Window Treatments" with sibling domains for the 818, 805, 310, 626,
858, and 949 area codes. It launched ~11 years after 805shutters.com
established the "805 Shutters" name in this market, has no physical presence in
Ventura County, and publishes fabricated review/experience claims.
