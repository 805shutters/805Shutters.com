# Commercial outreach templates

Use these as starting scripts. Keep every send short and specific, then log the
touch and next action in the private CRM or internal lead tracker.

## Property manager phone script

Hi, this is Kenneth Hill with 805 Shutters. We help property managers with commercial
shade and blind replacements for offices, vacancies, common areas, and glare or
privacy complaints.

I am not calling about a specific emergency. I am trying to find out who handles
window-covering vendors for your properties and whether you already have a good
resource.

If no:

We are offering a free commercial shade audit. It is a quick walkthrough to flag
damaged coverings, glare, privacy, heat, and safety issues, then we send a
priority replacement plan. Who would be the right person to send that to?

If yes:

That makes sense. If your current vendor ever gets backed up, can I send over
our capability sheet as a backup option?
