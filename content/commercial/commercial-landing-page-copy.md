## FAQ

### Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We can help with vacancy turns, damaged coverings, common areas, office
suites, and phased replacement plans.

### Can you quote from plans?

For some projects, yes. For most replacement projects, a site measure is still
needed before final pricing and ordering.

### Do you install commercial roller shades?

Yes. Roller shades, solar shades, blackout shades, motorized shades, vertical
blinds, and replacement blinds are all good commercial fits depending on the
space.

### Can you handle several rooms or buildings in phases?

Yes. We can break a project into priority areas so the buyer can control budget
and timing.
