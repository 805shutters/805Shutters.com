# 805 Shutters commercial capability sheet

## Commercial window coverings for Ventura County

805 Shutters provides commercial window coverings for offices, storefronts,
schools, medical spaces, property managers, tenant improvements, and shared
commercial spaces.

Primary work:

- Commercial roller shades.
- Solar shades for glare and heat control.
- Blackout shades for privacy, conference rooms, classrooms, and treatment rooms.
- Motorized shades and hard-to-reach window solutions.
- Vertical blinds and replacement blinds.
- Multi-window replacement programs.
- Safety-conscious cordless or inaccessible-cord options.
- Measuring, product ordering, delivery coordination, and installation.

## Free commercial shade audit

The audit reviews:

- Glare and sun exposure.
- Privacy and visibility from outside.
- Heat and comfort complaints.
- Damaged or outdated coverings.
- Cord/safety concerns.
- Phased replacement priorities.
- Rough budget range before final measure.

## Information needed for a quote

- Project address.
- Window count or plan set.
- Product preference, if known.
- Desired light/privacy level.
- Any motorization requirement.
- Access constraints or install-hour restrictions.
- Target install date.
- Billing and decision-maker contact.
