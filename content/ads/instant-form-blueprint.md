# Meta instant form blueprint

Date: 2026-06-01

## Form name

```text
805 Shutters - Free Consultation - Higher Intent
```

## Form type

Higher intent.

Use the review screen to reduce junk leads. Volume is less valuable than phone
numbers from people who are actually considering a project.

## Intro

Headline:

```text
Free Window Treatment Consultation
```

Body:

```text
Tell us what you want to update and 805 Shutters will follow up about custom
shutters, shades, blinds, or commercial window coverings for your home or
business.
```

## Contact fields

- First name.
- Last name.
- Phone number.
- Email.
- City.

## Custom questions

Question 1:

```text
What are you interested in?
```

Options:

- Shutters.
- Shades.
- Blinds.
- Commercial window coverings.
- Not sure yet.

Question 2:

```text
Where is the project?
```

Options:

- Camarillo.
- Thousand Oaks.
- Ventura.
- Oxnard.
- Simi Valley.
- Moorpark.
- Ojai.
- Other nearby city.

Question 3:

```text
When are you hoping to get started?
```

Options:

- As soon as possible.
- Within 30 days.
- 1-3 months.
- Just comparing options.

Question 4:

```text
Is this for a home or business?
```

Options:

- Home.
- Business.
- Both.

## Privacy policy

Use the live site privacy policy if one exists. If not, create one before
launching instant forms. Meta lead forms require a privacy policy URL.
