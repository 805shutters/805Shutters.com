# Nextdoor recommendation request templates

Use these before launching paid Nextdoor ads. The goal is 3-5 recommendations
from real local customers so paid visitors see social proof on the page.

Nextdoor page:
`https://nextdoor.com/page/805-shutters-and-shades-flint-mi/`

## Text message

Hi [First Name], this is 805 Shutters. We are getting our Nextdoor page ready
for local Ventura County neighbors. If you were happy with your shutters,
shades, or blinds, would you mind leaving us a quick recommendation here?

https://nextdoor.com/page/805-shutters-and-shades-flint-mi/

Thank you, we really appreciate it.

## Who to ask first

- Recent customers with finished installation photos.
- Customers who already gave positive feedback by text, email, Yelp, or phone.
- Camarillo, Thousand Oaks, Ventura, and Oxnard customers because those cities
  match the first paid ad copy.
- Avoid cold or unresolved customers until their project issue is closed.
