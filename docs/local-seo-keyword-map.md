## Content pattern for every city page

Use this structure so pages feel local and useful instead of templated:

1. H1 with product plus city.
2. Opening paragraph naming the city and product naturally.
3. Product fit: shutters, shades, blinds, motorization, commercial if relevant.
4. Local proof: years of local experience, family-owned, reviews, nearby cities.
5. Room/use cases: living rooms, bedrooms, sliding doors, offices, storefronts.
6. Internal links to parent service, gallery/recent projects, reviews, contact.
7. FAQ with 3 short questions tied to that service and city.
