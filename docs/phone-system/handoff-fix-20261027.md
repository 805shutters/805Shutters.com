# Forwarded staff pickup repair — October 27, 2026

## Outcome and scope

The 08:32 Pacific staff call answered but its asynchronous answering-machine-detection callback received HTTP409. The deployed v35 handler required ordinary Voice webhook To/From fields before reaching AMD handling. Twilio's AsyncAmdStatusCallback contains AccountSid, CallSid, AnsweredBy and MachineDetectionDuration instead.

The corrected Supabase and standalone handlers authenticate the signed account callback and require its CallSid to match the already-bound staff offer. Ordinary URL/status callbacks retain their To/From checks. A valid human answer continues through the existing handoff: cancel the other offer, route staff and customer to the same conference, detach AI, and mark human only after participant joins. Machine, unknown, expired, canceled, and ended offers do not gain ownership.

This is a phone Edge release; no website UI, schema, account configuration, greeting, recording, SMS, or customer data changes are part of the patch.
