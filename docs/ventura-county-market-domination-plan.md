# Ventura County Market Plan

Goal: make 805 Shutters the first company homeowners and businesses think of, find, trust, and contact for custom shutters, shades, blinds, drapery, exterior shades, and commercial window coverings in Ventura County.

## Core Thesis

The number one quote path is Google Search plus Google Maps. People search for terms like "custom shutters near me", "window coverings Ventura County", "plantation shutters Camarillo", "blinds Thousand Oaks", and "window shades Ventura". They compare the map pack, reviews, photos, websites, and quote/contact paths before calling or booking.

ShowMeLocal and other directories are citation support. They are not the growth engine.

## Positioning

805 Shutters should own this local category with one clear promise:

> Local Ventura County custom window coverings, measured correctly, installed professionally, and compared in one free consultation.

Primary proof points:

- Family-owned local company.
- 30+ years local experience.
- Custom shutters, shades, blinds, drapery, exterior shades, and commercial coverings in one appointment.
- Free in-home consultation.
- Fast phone response and appointment booking.
- Real local install photos by city and product.
- Review proof from Google, Yelp, BBB, Houzz, and other trusted surfaces.

## First Move

Do this first: build the Google quote machine before buying more traffic.

That means:

1. Fully optimize Google Business Profile.
2. Make the website convert Google visitors into booked consultations.
3. Build the first city-service SEO cluster around Camarillo, Thousand Oaks, Ventura, Oxnard, and Simi Valley.
4. Launch a small Google Search campaign only after tracking is verified.
5. Start a review-request process after every completed install and every good consultation.

## 14-Day Sprint

### Day 1-2: Google Business Profile

Actions:

- Primary category: Window Treatment Store.
- Secondary categories if available: Blinds Shop, Shutter Store, Curtain Supplier and Maker, Interior Designer, Contractor.
- Services: custom shutters, plantation shutters, window shades, roller shades, motorized shades, custom blinds, drapery, exterior shades, commercial roller shades, window covering installation.
- Service areas: Camarillo, Thousand Oaks, Ventura, Oxnard, Simi Valley, Moorpark, Ojai, Santa Rosa Valley, Port Hueneme, Santa Paula, Oak Park, Fillmore.
- Website link: `https://www.805shutters.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic_gbp&utm_campaign=gbp_website`
- Appointment link: `https://www.805shutters.com/book-consultation/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic_gbp&utm_campaign=gbp_appointment`
- Add 25+ photos: shutters, shades, blinds, commercial, exterior shades, closeups, before/after, install photos.
- Add products/services inside GBP using local search terms.
- Add Q&A questions customers actually ask.
- Post weekly: recent install, product comparison, free consultation, commercial shade audit.

Definition of done:

- Profile is complete.
- Photos are current.
- Appointment link goes directly to booking.
- Phone and website links are tracked.
- Review request link is ready.

### Day 3-5: Website Conversion

Actions:

- Make every high-intent page point to `/book-consultation/` and phone click.
- Add "Free In-Home Consultation" above the fold on service pages.
- Add real local proof near the CTA: review rating, years local, cities served, product range.
- Add a clear "compare shutters, shades, and blinds in one appointment" message.
- Add city/service links on the homepage and consultation page.
- Verify lead forms, phone click tracking, thank-you page, and booking flow.

Definition of done:

- A Google visitor can call, book, or request a quote in under 10 seconds.
- Every lead source carries UTM/page path into the CRM.

### Day 11-14: Review Engine

Actions:

- Create a standard review request SMS/email.
- Ask every happy customer for a Google review first.
- Ask for Yelp/Houzz only after Google.
- Save the review request link in the CRM.
- Track review asks and outcomes by customer.

Target:

- 4-8 new Google reviews per month until 805 Shutters is the obvious map-pack choice.

## Paid Search Launch

Start only after lead and phone tracking are verified.

Campaign structure:

- Campaign 1: Custom Shutters
- Campaign 2: Window Shades
- Campaign 3: Custom Blinds
- Campaign 4: Free Consultation
- Campaign 5: Commercial Window Coverings

Initial budget:

- Start at $50-$100/day.
- Run only Ventura County plus tight radius around service cities.
- Use phrase/exact match first.
- Avoid Display, Performance Max, broad national traffic, and cheap directory placements until the account has conversion data.

Best starting keywords:

- custom shutters near me
- plantation shutters Ventura County
- shutters Camarillo
- shutters Thousand Oaks

## Measurement

Weekly scorecard:

- Google Business Profile calls.
- GBP website clicks.
- GBP appointment clicks.
- Organic leads by page.
- Paid search leads.
- Phone clicks.
- Form submissions.
- Booked consultations.
- Quote close rate.
- Revenue by lead source.
- New Google reviews.
- Map-pack rankings for top city/service searches.

Success benchmark:

- First 30 days: prove tracking and get consistent booked consultations.
- First 60 days: improve top city rankings and review velocity.
- First 90 days: scale Google Search Ads and add project pages from completed installs.
- First 6 months: be top 3 in Google Maps for the primary Ventura County city/service searches.

## What Not To Do

- Do not spend serious budget on ShowMeLocal advertising unless they prove Ventura County calls/leads.
- Do not run broad Facebook ads as the main acquisition channel.
- Do not send paid traffic to a generic homepage if a consultation page or city/product page matches the search.
- Do not build thin duplicate city pages without local proof.
- Do not scale ads until phone, form, booking, and CRM attribution are clean.
